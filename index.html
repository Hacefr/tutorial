Chapter V

How to Use QOL Commands

You can do these commands:

  • /msg <playername> — This tells the targeted players a message!
  • /pettrust <playername> — This allows you to share pets!
  • /color <color or hex> — Color like red, green, orange, etc. or hex codes to have a custom username color!
  • /hub — Takes you to the hub.
  • /leave — Quickly leave minigames.
  • /pay <playername> <amount> — You can share and give money to your friends!
  • /gravetrust <playername> — This allows the trusted players to claim your grave for you!
  • /vop — For transparency! You can see all players with operator permissions at all times.
